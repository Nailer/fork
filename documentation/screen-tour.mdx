## Screens not pictured

| Screen | Route | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Launch | `/` | The Fork mark draws itself for about 0.85 s, then routes to `/onboarding` or `/home`. |
| New decision | `/new` | The full composer with optional context fields and status banners. |
| Paywall | `/paywall?reason=…` | A modal that shows the **real** monthly price from RevenueCat. Take this screenshot yourself with RevenueCat reachable. |
| Settings | `/settings` | Plan, restore, manage subscription, delete data, replay the intro. |
| Missing decision | `+not-found`, or any decision route whose id no longer exists | *“This decision isn’t here anymore. It may have been deleted, or it was an unsaved draft.”* with **Back to home**. |
