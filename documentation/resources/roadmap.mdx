From the README and the submission notes:

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| **Server-side entitlement check** (RevenueCat REST API) before deep explorations | Stops a modified client from requesting `deep` for free, and enables a server-side weekly limit |
| **Store builds** with real App Store and Google Play products on the same `fork_pro` entitlement | Real revenue |
| **Journal sync** across devices (iCloud or a Google account) | Decisions currently live on one device |
| **Reminders** to revisit a decision | Closes the loop: did the path play out as assumed? |
| **Annual plan** and an introductory offer in the RevenueCat offering | Pricing flexibility (see [Extending Fork](/contributing/extending-fork)) |
| **Localisation** | UI copy and prompts in more languages |
