## Levels

| Level | `LEVEL_LABEL` | `LEVEL_VALUE` | Bar segments |
|---|---|---|---|
| `low` | Low | 1 | ▮▯▯ |
| `moderate` | Moderate | 2 | ▮▮▯ |
| `high` | High | 3 | ▮▮▮ |

`LEVEL_VALUE` is used **only** to compute the spread between paths. It’s never summed into a score.

## Dimensions

| Key | Label | Plan | Meaning (as told to the model) | low | moderate | high |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cost` | Cost | Free | how much money it takes | Low cost | Some cost | High cost |
| `time` | Time | Free | how much time it consumes | Little time | Some time | A lot of time |
| `flexibility` | Flexibility | Free | how many options it keeps open | Locks you in | Some room | Keeps options open |
| `risk` | Risk | Free | how much could go wrong | Low risk | Some risk | Higher risk |
| `effort` | Effort | Pro | how much work it takes | Light lift | Some effort | Heavy lift |
| `upside` | Potential upside | Pro | how much potential benefit it offers | Modest upside | Some upside | Big upside |
| `shortTerm` | Short-term impact | Pro | how much changes in the near term | Small change now | Noticeable now | Big change now |
| `longTerm` | Long-term impact | Pro | how much it shapes what comes later | Fades over time | Lasting effect | Shapes what comes next |

## Exports

```ts
export type DimensionMeta = { key: DimensionKey; label: string; levels: Record<Level, string>; pro: boolean };

export const DIMENSIONS: Record<DimensionKey, DimensionMeta>;

export const LEVEL_LABEL: Record<Level, string>;
export const LEVEL_VALUE: Record<Level, number>;
```
