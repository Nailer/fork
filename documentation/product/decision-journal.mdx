The journal lists every **kept** decision (drafts don’t appear), newest update first.

## Layout

- Title: **Decision journal**, with a count (*“3 decisions kept”*).
- Free users see capacity, e.g. **3/5 on Free**. It turns gold when full.
- Decisions are grouped by when they were last updated:
  - **This week**: updated in the last 7 days
  - **Earlier**: everything older

Each row (`DecisionRow`) shows:

| Part | Example |
|---|---|
| Decision title | *New laptop now, or one more year?* |
| What you leaned toward | *Path C · Fix it up, then decide*, or *Still deciding* |
| Your note (journal only) | *“Try the cheap fix first; revisit in March.”* |
| Date | *Sep 28, 2026* (formatted in your locale), plus *· Sample* for the sample decision |

A small Fork mark highlights the side of the chosen path.

## Actions

| Action | How | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Open** | Tap a row | Opens the fork with the *You leaned toward…* banner |
| **Explore again** | Open → *Explore this decision again* | Copies your words and context into the composer for a fresh exploration. The kept decision is unchanged. |
| **Update** | Open → **Your fork** → change the choice or note → **Update decision** | Updates `chosen`, `note` and `updatedAt` |
| **Delete** | Trash icon on the row → confirm | Removes the decision and its note from this device |

The delete confirmation reads: *“‘New laptop now, or one more year?’ and your note will be removed from this device.”*

## Empty state

With nothing kept yet, the journal shows a drawn Fork mark, **“Your decision journal is empty”**, and three reasons to keep decisions:

- See why you chose what you chose, months later.
- Revisit the paths you didn’t take.
- Notice patterns in how you decide.

**Explore a decision** takes you to the New decision screen.

## Limits

| | Free | Fork Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Kept decisions | **5** | Unlimited |
| Re-saving an existing decision | Always allowed | Always allowed |
| Unsaved drafts kept | 3 newest | 3 newest |

Home also shows the three most recent kept decisions under **Recent decisions**.

## Storage

The journal lives only in local storage (AsyncStorage key `fork:v1`). There’s no account, backup or sync. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data removes it. See [State and persistence](/architecture/state).
