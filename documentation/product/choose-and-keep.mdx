## The recap

| Section | Content |
|---|---|
| **Decision** | Your original description, word for word |
| **Paths explored** | Every path with its letter badge and **Explored** / **Not opened**. Tap to open it. |
| **Where the paths differ most** | Up to 3 dimensions with a non-zero spread, taken from your selected comparison dimensions, e.g. *Cost: A: high cost · B: low cost · C: some cost* |
| **Most important tradeoffs** | The first tradeoff of each path |
| **Questions worth answering** | The decision-level `questions` (1–5) |

## Choosing

Under **Which path are you leaning toward?** there’s one radio option per path plus **Still deciding**. The hint reads: *“Fork doesn’t pick for you. This is your call — you can change it later.”*

Below it, **Why I’m choosing this** is an optional note (up to **600 characters**) with the placeholder *“Optional — a note to your future self”*.

## Keeping it

The footer button changes with state:

| State | Button |
|---|---|
| New decision, journal has room | **Keep this decision** |
| Already kept | **Update decision** |
| Free user, journal full (5 kept), and this decision isn’t kept yet | **Unlock unlimited history** (gold, with a lock), which opens the paywall with `reason=save` |

The button stays disabled until you pick an option. Keeping a decision:

1. stores `chosen` as `{ kind: 'scenario', scenarioId }` or `{ kind: 'undecided' }`,
2. trims and stores the note,
3. sets `saved: true` and `updatedAt: now`,
4. plays a success haptic and fires `decision_saved` with `undecided` and `withNote`.

<Info>
Re-saving a decision that’s already in your journal is **always allowed**, even when the Free journal is full. Only *adding* a sixth decision needs Pro.
</Info>

## Confirmation

A full-screen confirmation shows **Decision kept.**, with a tick in the chosen path’s colour:

- For a path: *“You’re leaning toward ‘Fix it up, then decide’. It’s in your history whenever you want to revisit it.”*
- For Still deciding: *“You’re still deciding — that’s fine. Come back to it any time from your history.”*

Then either **Go to my decisions** (opens the journal) or **Back to home**.
